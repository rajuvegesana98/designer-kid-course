Foundation
Why Colour Matters
Colour is not decoration. It communicates before words do.
🧠
Emotion
Blue = trust. Red = urgency. Green = success. Colour sets the mood before the user reads a word.
👁️
Attention
A red "Buy Now" button on a white page grabs attention. Colour guides the eye to what matters.
🏷️
Brand Identity
Coca-Cola red, WhatsApp green, Spotify green — you recognise brands by colour before the logo.
💡 Think of colour like tone of voice — a calm blue says "you're safe here", a bold red says "act now". Same message, different feeling.

Warm vs Cool

Warm (reds, oranges, yellows) feel energetic. Cool (blues, greens, purples) feel calm.

Tint · Shade · Tone

Tint = colour + white (lighter). Shade = colour + black (darker). Tone = colour + grey (muted).

Pairing Colours
Colour Harmony
Rules that make colour combinations feel intentional, not random.
Complementary
Opposite colours on the wheel — high contrast, very eye-catching. Example: blue + orange. Use for CTAs and highlights.
Analogous
Neighbours on the wheel — calm and harmonious. Example: blue + blue-green + green. Great for backgrounds and UI surfaces.
Triadic
Three colours equally spaced on the wheel — vibrant balance. Example: red + yellow + blue. Works well for playful, energetic products.
💡 The 60-30-10 rule — 60% dominant colour (background), 30% secondary (surfaces), 10% accent (buttons, highlights). Almost every good UI uses this split.

Practice
Building a UI Palette
Every great product UI has just 3–4 core colours used consistently.
Brand / Primary
The main colour — buttons, links, key actions. Should feel like "you".
Neutral / Background
White, grey, near-black. Takes up 60%+ of the screen. Never compete with brand.
Semantic Colours
Red = error. Green = success. Yellow = warning. These are universal — don't fight convention.
Accent / Highlight
A supporting colour for variety — used sparingly (10% rule) for badges, tags, hover states.
Real World
Colour in Famous Apps
Every colour choice was intentional — there's always a reason.
🟢 WhatsApp — Green
Green = communication, go, positive. Friendly and approachable. Stands out on a phone home screen.
🔵 Facebook/LinkedIn — Blue
Blue = trust, reliability, professionalism. Perfect for platforms where people share personal info.
⚫ Swiggy — Orange
Orange = appetite, energy, speed. Warm colours make food look more tempting and ordering feel fast.
🟣 Zomato — Red
Red = urgency + appetite. Literally the colour of food. Gets you hungry and makes you act fast.

Hands-On Activity
Pick a Colour Palette
Activity · 20 min work · 10 min share · 10 min Q&A
  1. Pick any app you use daily (Swiggy, YouTube, Instagram, Paytm).
  2. Write down the 3 main colours used in that app.
  3. For each colour, guess WHY they chose it — emotion? function? brand?
  4. Now create a mini palette for YOUR app idea: 1 brand colour, 1 neutral, 1 accent.
  5. Present your palette with the reason behind each colour choice.
