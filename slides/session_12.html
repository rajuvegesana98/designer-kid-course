Colour Harmonies
Picking Colours That Work Together
Not all colour combos look good. These three rules make it easy.
Complementary
Opposite sides of the wheel. High contrast, eye-catching. Good for CTAs and highlights.
Analogous
Colours next to each other. Harmonious and calm. Great for backgrounds and app themes.
Triadic
Three colours evenly spaced. Vibrant and balanced. Use one dominant + two accents.
Contrast & Accessibility
Can Everyone Read Your Design?
1 in 12 men and 1 in 200 women have some form of colour blindness. WCAG says body text needs 4.5:1 contrast ratio.
✓ Good Contrast
Dark text on white — easy to read
Light text on dark — clear
White on purple — passes WCAG AA
✗ Poor Contrast
Grey text on white — hard to read
Yellow text on yellow-ish
Pink on red — invisible
Tool tip: Use the Contrast Checker on coolors.co or the Figma "Stark" plugin to test your colours.
UI Palette Structure
A UI Palette Has 3 Roles
Don't pick colours randomly. Assign each colour a job.
🎨
Brand / Primary
Your main colour — used for buttons, links, key actions. Pick one. Don't use too many.
⬜
Neutral / Surface
Backgrounds, cards, borders, text. Usually whites, greys, or near-blacks.
🚦
Semantic / Status
Green = success, Red = error, Yellow = warning, Blue = info. These have universal meaning.
