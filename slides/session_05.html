Information Architecture

What Is IA?

IA is how you organise, label, and connect content so users can find things without thinking hard.

📁
Organisation
Grouping related content into logical categories users recognise
🏷️
Labelling
Naming sections and pages in the user's vocabulary, not yours
🔍
Navigation
The pathways and menus that connect pages together
💡 Think of IA like a library. Books are content. Shelves are categories. Signs are labels. The floor plan is navigation.

Common Pitfalls

IA Mistakes That Break UX

😵
Too Many Top-Level Items
Navigation with 12 items overwhelms. Aim for 5–7 max at the top level.
🗣️
Company Jargon as Labels
"Resources" vs "Guides" — use the word your user would say, not internal terminology.
🕳️
Orphan Pages
Pages that exist but aren't linked from navigation. Users can't find them organically.
🪆
Burying Important Content
Key info hidden 4 levels deep. Important content should be max 2–3 clicks away.
Wireframing

What Is a Wireframe?

A wireframe is a low-fidelity blueprint — boxes and lines showing layout, not colour or style. The goal is to nail structure before spending time on visuals.

What wireframes ARE
✅ Layout and spacing blueprints
✅ Content placement decisions
✅ Navigation flow sketches
✅ Quick communication tools for the team
What wireframes are NOT
❌ Final visual designs
❌ Colour/typography decisions
❌ High-fidelity prototypes
❌ Pixel-perfect mockups

Fidelity Spectrum

Low → Mid → High Fidelity

✏️
Low Fidelity
Hand sketches or basic digital boxes. Fast, disposable. Use for brainstorming and early alignment.
Tools: Paper, Whiteboards
🖥️
Mid Fidelity
Digital wireframes with real content, spacing, and component placeholders. The design brief standard.
Tools: Figma, Whimsical
🎨
High Fidelity
Near-final mockup with colour, typography, and real imagery. Used for stakeholder sign-off.
Tools: Figma, Sketch
As fidelity rises, changes cost more time. Low-fi decisions are cheap; high-fi changes are expensive.

Hands-On Activity
🗂️ Sitemap + Wireframe Sprint
Build the IA and a wireframe for a simple cooking app — 20 mins activity · 10 mins share
Pick a simple app: recipe browser, fitness tracker, or event planner
List 8–12 pages/screens the app needs on sticky notes or paper
Group and arrange them into a sitemap (3 levels max)
Pick one page and sketch a low-fi wireframe — boxes only, no colour
