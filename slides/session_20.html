Interview Skill
The 5-Minute Walkthrough
Every design interview includes "walk me through a project." Here's the structure that works:
1m

Set the context

"I worked on X. The problem was Y. The user was Z."

1m

Show your research

"I talked to 5 users / surveyed 20 people / analysed 3 competitors and found..."

1m

Show your process

"I started with a persona, mapped the user flow, then wireframed 3 versions."

1m

Show the solution

"The final design solved X because..." — 2–3 key screens only, not all of them.

1m

Reflect honestly

"If I had more time I'd test X. What I'd do differently is Y." This wins points — it shows growth mindset.

Career Options
Where UX Can Take You
🎨
Product Designer
End-to-end — research, wireframes, visual design, prototyping, and handoff. Most common title at tech startups. Salary range in India: ₹4–18 LPA entry to mid-level.
🔍
UX Researcher
Focused purely on understanding users — interviews, surveys, usability tests. Usually in larger companies (Swiggy, Flipkart, banks). Great for people who love talking to users.
💻
UI Developer / Design Engineer
Bridges design and code. Builds UI components in React/HTML/CSS from Figma. High demand as companies want designers who can implement. Learn basic HTML/CSS alongside design.
✍️
UX Writer / Content Designer
Writes the words inside products — button labels, error messages, onboarding copy. Rare skill, low competition. If you're strong at writing and design, this is a hidden gem path.

The Real Path
Getting Your First Opportunity
Start here

Internships

Best way to get experience. Apply to startups on LinkedIn, Internshala. Don't wait for perfect — apply now with what you have.

Freelance for local businesses

Redesign a local restaurant's menu app, a coaching centre's website. Small projects = real portfolio pieces.

Open source contributions

Find apps with bad UX, write a case study proposing improvements. Many designers got hired this way.

Build your presence

Post on LinkedIn weekly

Share what you're designing, what you're learning. Recruiters search LinkedIn daily. Consistency beats perfection.

Agencies vs Startups

Agencies teach speed and client management. Startups teach ownership and product thinking. Both are valid first jobs.

Join design communities

Designer Hangout Slack, ADPList mentorship, local UX meetups. The design world is small — relationships matter.
