Feedback Culture

Design Critique — Done Right

Giving feedback
Be specific: "this button is too small" not "I don't like it"
Refer to the user: "a new user would miss this"
Ask questions: "what were you trying to do here?"
Separate taste from usability
Receiving feedback
Listen first — don't defend
Ask "why?" to understand context
You don't have to act on everything
Thank the reviewer — it takes effort
Watch Out

Common Mistakes at This Stage

Only showing polished finals
Recruiters want to see how you think, not just what it looks like at the end. Show the messy middle.
No explanation of decisions
"I made it look nice" is not a design reason. Tie every choice back to the user or a problem.
Copying trends blindly
Glassmorphism doesn't solve problems. Design for the user first, aesthetics second.
Not documenting as you go
You'll forget why you made that choice 3 weeks from now. Write it down while it's fresh.
