Course Structure

Four weeks. One complete designer.

Each week is a focused sprint. You come in knowing nothing; you leave Week 4 with a portfolio and the language to talk confidently about your process in interviews.

01
Week One

Foundations of UX

You think like a user before you pick up a pen. Research, empathy mapping, personas, user flows, information architecture, and lo-fi wireframes.

  • What is UX Design?
  • User Research Methods
  • Empathy Mapping
  • Persona & User Flow
  • IA & Wireframing
02
Week Two

Design Craft

You make it look and feel right. Visual design, real UI components, a working Figma prototype, and your first usability test with feedback from real people.

  • Visual Design Basics
  • UI Components & Patterns
  • Prototyping Basics
  • Usability Testing
  • Portfolio Review
03
Week Three

Advanced UI

You go deeper on the craft that separates good work from great work — colour science, typographic system, inclusive design, and mobile-first thinking.

  • Colour Theory for UI
  • Colour & UI Design
  • Typography in UI
  • Accessibility
  • Mobile-First Design
04
Week Four

Professional Practice

You prepare for the job. Handoff specs, motion design, design systems, and a portfolio case study — finished with a final presentation to a live audience.

  • Handoff & Dev Collab
  • Motion & Micro-interactions
  • Design Systems
  • Portfolio & Case Studies
  • Final Presentations
Skills You'll Build

Design thinking, not just design tools.

Tools change. The ability to identify a user problem, frame a solution, and communicate your reasoning to stakeholders — that's what gets you hired and keeps you growing.

🔍
User ResearchInterviews, surveys, empathy maps, affinity diagrams — the foundations of every good design decision.
✏️
Wireframing & IASitemaps, lo-fi wireframes, content hierarchy — structure before style.
🎨
Visual DesignColour theory, typography, hierarchy, contrast — making interfaces that communicate without words.
⚡
PrototypingFigma clickable prototypes, interaction design — making ideas testable before a single line of code.
♿
AccessibilityWCAG standards, inclusive design, colour contrast — designing for everyone, not just the average user.
📱
Mobile-FirstResponsive thinking, touch targets, thumb zone — designing for the device most people actually use.
🤝
Dev HandoffWriting specs, Figma inspect, QA review — so your designs actually ship the way you designed them.
🗂️
Design SystemsTokens, components, style guides, Atomic Design — building at scale without reinventing the wheel.
💼
Portfolio BuildingCase study structure, platforms, writing tips — presenting your process as compellingly as your final designs.
