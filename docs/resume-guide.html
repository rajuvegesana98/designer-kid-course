The 1-Page Rule

For junior designers with under 3 years of experience, your resume must be exactly one page. Two-page resumes from junior candidates signal poor editing and low ability to prioritise — two things designers must do well. If you can't edit your resume to one page, it's a warning sign to recruiters.

Writing Strong Bullet Points

The formula: Action Verb + Task + Result

✅ Strong bullets

  • Redesigned checkout flow reducing drop-off by 35% based on 5 usability test sessions
  • Conducted 8 user interviews and synthesised findings into 3 design principles that shaped the MVP
  • Built a 12-screen Figma prototype that was approved in first stakeholder review

❌ Weak bullets

  • Helped with the design of the app
  • Worked on user research and wireframes
  • Good at Figma and creative thinking

ATS (Applicant Tracking System) Tips

Most companies use ATS software to filter resumes before a human sees them. A resume rejected by ATS never reaches the recruiter.

  • Use simple, standard section headings: "Experience", "Skills", "Education" — not "My Story" or "What I've Built."
  • Include exact keywords from the job description. If the JD says "Figma", your resume must say "Figma" — not "prototyping tool" or "design software."
  • Avoid tables, columns, headers, and text boxes in Word/PDF. ATS often can't parse them. Single-column layout is safest.
  • Submit as PDF unless the company specifically asks for Word.
  • Don't put important content in the header or footer — some ATS tools skip those sections.
Before submitting, paste your resume text into a plain text editor. If it reads logically, ATS can parse it. If it's a mess of symbols and broken lines, fix the formatting.

Resume Checklist

  • ✦ Exactly one page — no exceptions for junior roles
  • ✦ Portfolio URL is clickable and works
  • ✦ Every bullet starts with a past-tense action verb (Designed, Conducted, Built, Reduced)
  • ✦ At least one bullet per project has a quantified result
  • ✦ Skills section lists tools, not soft skills
  • ✦ No photo on resume (standard in India for product companies — check the company's JD if unsure)
  • ✦ No "References available on request" — assumed, takes up space
  • ✦ Consistent date format throughout (Month Year or MM/YYYY)
  • ✦ Proofread by at least one other person
  • ✦ Saved as PDF: "FirstLast_UXDesigner_Resume.pdf"
