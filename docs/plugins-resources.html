Plugins & Resources Guide

The complete toolkit for the 4-week UI/UX course — every tool, plugin, font, and reference curated for beginners.

Core Design Tools

Figma Free tier
figma.com
Primary design and prototyping tool. All course activities are Figma-compatible. Free tier supports unlimited files with limited editors.
FigJam Free tier
figma.com/figjam
Online whiteboard included with Figma. Used for empathy mapping, affinity diagrams, and brainstorming sessions.
Whimsical Free tier
whimsical.com
Fast wireframing and flowcharts. Great for user flow diagrams and sitemaps in Sessions 04 and 05.
Canva Free
canva.com
Presentation and portfolio layouts. Use for building case study presentations if not using Figma.

Figma Plugins (install from Figma → Plugins menu)

Contrast Free
Checks WCAG colour contrast ratios directly on selected layers. Essential for Sessions 11, 12, and 14 (accessibility).
Unsplash Free
Insert real photos from Unsplash directly into Figma frames. Use for adding realistic imagery to wireframes and mockups.
Iconify Free
Access 150,000+ open-source icons across 100+ icon sets. Covers Material, Feather, Tabler, and more.
Lorem Ipsum Free
Generates placeholder text at any word count. Fills text layers quickly during wireframing sessions.
Autoflow Free
Draws flow arrows between frames automatically. Useful for creating user flow diagrams in Session 04.
Component Inspector Free
Shows component usage across a file. Helps students understand their own component structure during Sessions 07 and 18.
Design Tokens Free
Export Figma styles as JSON design tokens. Pairs with Session 18 (Design Systems) to show tokens in practice.
Wireframe Free
Pre-built low-fidelity UI kit for Figma. Drag-and-drop wireframe components for rapid prototyping.

Typography Resources

Google Fonts Free
fonts.google.com
1,500+ open-source fonts. Recommended pairs for course: Syne + DM Sans (used in slide decks), Inter + Source Serif, Outfit + Merriweather.
Fontpair Free
fontpair.co
Curated Google Fonts pairings with live previews. Referenced in Session 13 (Typography).
Type Scale Free
typescale.com
Generates modular type scales (Minor Third, Major Third, Perfect Fourth, etc.) with CSS output.
Fonts In Use Free
fontsinuse.com
Real-world typography examples categorised by industry. Great for Session 13 inspiration research.

Colour Resources

Coolors Free tier
coolors.co
Generate and explore colour palettes. Lock a colour and auto-generate harmonious combinations. Sessions 11 and 12.
Colour & Contrast Free
colour-contrast.com
WCAG contrast ratio checker with clear AA/AAA labels. Referenced in Session 14 (Accessibility).
Happy Hues Free
happyhues.co
Curated palettes shown in real UI context. Students can see exactly how a colour scheme looks applied to a webpage.
Huemint Free
huemint.com
AI-powered colour palette generator for UI, illustration, and brand use cases.

Accessibility Tools

WebAIM Contrast Checker Free
webaim.org/resources/contrastchecker
The most referenced WCAG contrast tool. Shows AA/AAA pass/fail for normal and large text.
Who Can Use Free
whocanuse.com
Shows how many people with different vision conditions can read a colour combination. Powerful for Session 14 empathy building.
WAVE Free
wave.webaim.org
Paste any URL and get a full accessibility audit with visual markers. Used in Session 14 activity.
NoCoffee Vision Simulator Free
Chrome extension that simulates various visual impairments on any webpage. Essential for empathy in Session 14.

UI Inspiration & Research

Mobbin Free tier
mobbin.com
Real app screenshots organised by screen type (onboarding, checkout, etc.). The #1 reference for UI pattern research.
Dribbble Free
dribbble.com
Designer portfolio platform. Browse for visual inspiration. Note: Dribbble is aspirational, not always functional UI.
Behance Free
behance.net
Full case studies with process documentation. Best for studying how designers communicate their thinking (S19).
Laws of UX Free
lawsofux.com
25 psychological principles behind good UX. Each law has examples and references. A reference students keep for their career.

Free Icon Libraries

Heroicons Free
heroicons.com
230+ SVG icons by the Tailwind CSS team. Outline and solid variants. Copy as SVG or JSX.
Feather Icons Free
feathericons.com
Simply beautiful open-source icons. 280 icons with consistent 24px grid. Great for minimal UI projects.
Google Material Icons Free
fonts.google.com/icons
2,500+ icons with 5 visual styles. The standard for Android and web Material Design projects.
Tabler Icons Free
tabler-icons.io
4,000+ hand-crafted SVG icons. Consistent stroke weight. Excellent for dashboard and admin UI work.

Image Resources

Unsplash Free
unsplash.com
High-quality, royalty-free photography. Use for mockups and case study visuals. No attribution required.
Pexels Free
pexels.com
Free stock photos and videos. Good variety of Indian-context lifestyle imagery for local UX projects.
unDraw Free
undraw.co
Open-source SVG illustrations with customisable brand colour. Great for onboarding screens and empty states.
Storyset Free
storyset.com
Editable, animated illustration packs. Download SVG or PNG with your brand colour applied.

Learning & Reference

Nielsen Norman Group Free articles
nngroup.com
The gold standard UX research organisation. Free articles on every UX topic. Students should bookmark this immediately.
UX Collective Free
uxdesign.cc
Practitioner-written articles on design process, tools, and careers. Medium publication with excellent weekly content.
Figma Community Free
figma.com/community
Free templates, UI kits, and design systems from the global design community. Students can remix any file for learning.
Design Systems Repo Free
designsystemsrepo.com
Curated collection of public design systems (Material, Apple HIG, Atlassian, etc.). Referenced in Session 18.
