Course Overview

This course runs for 4 weeks, 5 sessions per week, with each session lasting 1 hour. The structure is: 20 min content delivery → 20 min hands-on activity → 10 min share-out → 10 min Q&A. Students are beginners — no design software experience assumed.

Tools needed: Figma (free tier), paper + pen for sketching, browser for viewing slide decks.

Facilitation Tips

Slide navigation: Use left/right arrow keys or click the nav dots at the bottom. Progress bar at the top tracks your position.

Live demos: Sessions 07, 12, 13, and 17 contain interactive CSS demos in the slides. Explain what's happening before letting students interact.

When students feel stuck: ask "what do you think the user is trying to do?" rather than giving the answer. Guide them to reason from the user's perspective.

Keep the energy high in Week 1. Students are often intimidated by "design". Emphasise that UX is problem-solving first, aesthetics second.

Common Questions & Answers

Do I need to know Figma to do this course?

No. The first two weeks use paper sketches. Figma is introduced in Week 2 Session 08 (Prototyping). Students with zero Figma experience will be fine — the activities are scaffolded.

Is this course only for people who want to be designers?

No. Product managers, developers, marketers, and founders all benefit from UX fluency. Encourage students from any background.

How is portfolio work evaluated?

There is no formal grading. The portfolio check in S10 and the final presentation in S20 are peer-reviewed. Emphasise process documentation over visual polish at the beginner stage.
